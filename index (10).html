My Story

I was the patient
nobody could reach.

I have been hearing impaired since I was a child. Every morning before school I took my hearing aids out and hid them. I was more afraid of being teased than of missing everything around me.

As an adult I walked into audiology offices and said "I'll think about it." Then I left. I did that for seven years. Not because the audiologists were wrong — because nobody ever met me where I actually was: scared, ashamed, and completely alone in something I didn't have the language for yet.

I built QuantumSparkCreative because I am the person who needed this and couldn't find it anywhere. Not a clinician with textbook knowledge — the actual person who lived inside it, and came out the other side with something worth sharing.

— Korey Bogle

"Hearing loss is not just a clinical problem. It is a relationship problem. A confidence problem. An identity problem. And none of that gets addressed in a 45-minute appointment."

How the Group Works

You don't heal in isolation.
You heal in community.

The cohort is different from 1:1 coaching in one critical way — you are surrounded by people living exactly what you are living, right now. That changes everything.

Week 1
The honest audit
Every person shares where they actually are. No performance. No "I'm fine." Just the truth — in a room where everyone else is holding the same truth.
Weeks 2–4
The work — shame, relationships, identity
We move through the stages together. The hiding. The conversations that need to happen. Korey leads; the group holds each other accountable in ways 1:1 coaching cannot replicate.
Weeks 5–8
Treatment, adjustment, what's next
Members starting treatment walk through the adjustment period together. Those not yet there build the roadmap together. Nobody is left behind.
Ongoing
You stay as long as you need
Month-to-month. No fixed end date. Members through the hardest part often stay to support newer members — which accelerates their own healing.
